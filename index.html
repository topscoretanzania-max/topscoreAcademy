Who we are

Positive media. Purposeful childhoods.

Little Disciple TV is a Christian online TV initiative based in Kimara Korogwe, Dar es Salaam, Tanzania. We bring together faith, education, talent development and character-building in a child-friendly digital space.

Our Vision

To raise a generation of children who know and love God, discover their talents, and positively influence their communities.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality Christian media that nurtures faith, develops talents, promotes learning, and builds character among children and youth.
